03 — research

Research

Where machine learning meets privacy and distributed systems.

Ongoing · Federated Learning

Federated Learning with GAN-Based Synthetic Data Augmentation

A privacy-preserving, non-IID federated learning system on CIFAR-10 — ViT-Lite clients with per-client Conditional GANs and server-side refinement.

Against a paired independent-FedAvg baseline across 3–10 clients, I showed the benefit is gated by label-skew severity — meaningful under severe skew, negligible under milder skew.
